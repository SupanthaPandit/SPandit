RESEARCH OPPORTUNITIES

Prospective Research Students & Postdoctoral Fellows

I welcome enquiries from motivated Master’s students, PhD applicants, and prospective postdoctoral researchers interested in theoretical computer science, particularly computational geometry, graph algorithms, approximation algorithms, combinatorial optimization, and distributed computing. Master’s research may combine theoretical analysis with implementation or experimental studies, whereas PhD and postdoctoral research generally focuses on foundational problems in algorithm design and complexity. A strong background in algorithms, data structures, discrete mathematics, and mathematical problem-solving is desirable. Interested candidates may email me with their CV, research interests, and relevant academic background. Prospective postdoctoral researchers may also consult the DAU University Postdoctoral Fellowship advertisement ↗.

01 / Introduction

About me

Biography

I am an Associate Professor at Dhirubhai Ambani University (DAU), Gandhinagar, Gujarat, India. I joined the institution in April 2019 and have served as Associate Professor since May 2025.

Previously, I was a SERB Indo-US Postdoctoral Fellow at Stony Brook University, working with Prof. Joseph S. B. Mitchell, and a SERB National Postdoctoral Fellow at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, working with Prof. Subhas C. Nandy. I received my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Ropar in 2017, under the supervision of Dr. Apurva Mudgal.

My primary interests lie in theoretical computer science, particularly geometric and combinatorial algorithms, approximation and optimization, and algorithms for distributed networks and mobile agents. I am also interested in intersections with machine learning and artificial intelligence.
